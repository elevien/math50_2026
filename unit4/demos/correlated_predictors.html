Marginal slope vs. the regression coefficient

$X_1\sim\text{Bernoulli}(0.5)$, $X_2\mid X_1\sim\text{Normal}(bX_1,1)$, $Y\mid(X_1,X_2)\sim\text{Normal}(\beta_1X_1+\beta_2X_2,\ 0.4^2)$. Left: $Y$ vs. the continuous predictor $X_2$, split by $X_1$. Right: $Y$ vs. the binary predictor $X_1$ (jittered), split by tercile of $X_2$. Increase $b$ to strengthen the confounding between the two predictors and pull the solid (within-group) and dashed (marginal) lines apart.
