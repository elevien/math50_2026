$p$-values and their relationship to confidence intervals

Rust: the null distribution $\text{Normal}(0,\text{se}^2)$ of $\hat\beta_1^\ast$; the shaded tails beyond $\pm|\hat\beta_1|$ have total area $p_v$. Blue: the estimated sample distribution $\text{Normal}(\hat\beta_1,\text{se}^2)$; the shaded middle $95\%$ is the confidence interval. $0$ falls outside the blue band exactly when the rust tails have area below $0.05$.
