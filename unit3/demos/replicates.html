Replicates vs. samples, and the sample distribution of $\hat\mu$

$Y \sim \text{Normal}(\mu,\sigma^2)$ with $\mu=0$, $\sigma=1.5$ fixed. The pale bars (top) are the $N$ individual samples $Y_i$ from the latest replicate; the solid bars (bottom) are $\hat\mu$ collected one per replicate, compared against the theoretical $\text{Normal}(\mu,\sigma^2/N)$ curve.
